# KaretomoSMP Official Modpack

**KaretomoSMP Official Modpack** は、Minecraftサーバー**「KaretomoSMP」**に参加するプレイヤーのために特別に構築・調整された、公式のオールインワン軽量化＆便利機能（QoL）Modパックです。

---

## 🎯 プロジェクトの目的
本Modパックの目的は、参加者全員がPCのスペックに関わらず、高FPSかつラグのないストレスフリーな環境でマルチプレイを楽しめるようにすることです。重くなりがちな拠点エリアや大人数でのイベント時でも、動作の落ち込みを抑え、最高のゲームプレイ体験を提供します。

---

## ✨ 主な特徴と魅力

### ⚡ 圧倒的なパフォーマンス向上
**Sodium**、**Lithium**、**FerriteCore**、**ModernFix** などの最先端Fabric軽量化Modを多数同梱。フレームレート（FPS）を大幅に向上させ、メモリ消費量やスタッター（カクつき）を極限まで削減します。

### 🛠️ 快適な操作性とUI拡張（QoL）
バニラ本来の雰囲気を壊すことなく、インベントリ整理機能や詳細なUI表示などの利便性向上機能をプラス。日々のプレイや作業効率が格段にアップします。

### 🔌 KaretomoSMPに完全最適化
サーバー側の仕様やプラグインに合わせて事前にチューニングされているため、Mod同士の競合や接続の不具合を心配することなく、安心して導入できます。

---

# KaretomoSMP Official Modpack (English)

**KaretomoSMP Official Modpack** is an official, all-in-one client-side optimization and Quality of Life (QoL) modpack specifically designed and fine-tuned for players on the **KaretomoSMP** Minecraft server.

---

## 🎯 Project Purpose
The primary goal of this modpack is to provide a seamless, high-performance, and lag-free gameplay experience for every community member—regardless of their PC hardware specifications. By optimizing client-side performance and streamlining everyday interactions, players can focus entirely on building, exploring, and collaborating together without technical distractions.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Highlights

### ⚡ Extreme Performance & FPS Boost
Powered by industry-leading Fabric optimization mods—including **Sodium**, **Lithium**, **FerriteCore**, and **ModernFix**—this pack significantly increases frame rates (FPS), minimizes frame drops, and drastically reduces RAM usage even in heavy multiplayer areas.

### 🛠️ Enhanced Quality of Life (QoL)
Includes subtle yet powerful usability improvements such as intuitive inventory sorting, enhanced tooltips, and useful UI overlays. These additions streamline your daily gameplay without breaking or altering the core Vanilla Minecraft feel.

### 🔌 Server-Tailored Compatibility
Every included mod has been thoroughly tested and verified for full compatibility with KaretomoSMP server settings and plugins, guaranteeing zero mod conflicts, fast loading times, and maximum connection stability.

---

## 🚀 How to Get Started
1. Download and install the modpack via your favorite launcher (Modrinth App, Prism Launcher, etc.).
2. Launch Minecraft with the allocated recommended RAM (2GB to 4GB is sufficient).
3. Connect to **KaretomoSMP** and enjoy a smooth, optimized Minecraft experience!
